HOW FAST I REPLY

a chart · idk what the methodology is · fig. 1
within an hour = you are wrong about a philosopher i like
two or three days = a normal, friendly message
two weeks = it deserves a thoughtful answer, so i freeze
never = you have attached a 40-page pdf with no note

The kinder the message the longer i take because i want to answer it properly and then i put it somewhere safe and then it is march.

before you write ~ a short list

  1. I do read everything. I am just slow. see fig. 1
  2. If it is about a post, say which post. I have written about a lot of things and i forget my own arguments.
  3. corrections are very welcome. I have been wrong in public before and will be again. I would rather know.
  4. Students asking about international law: yes.
  5. No politics arguments please
  6. (crossed out — who am i kidding. fine. politics arguments. but be interesting.)
  7. please do not send me a crypto newsletter. i do not know how you found me.
  8. found a broken link? you are owed one (1) favour, redeemable never, but sincerely offered.
