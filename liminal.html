The log

first post

12 august 1999 · 3:14 am · mood: awake

got the night contract at the annexe. eleven hours, four floors, one badge that opens most things. the day people leave at six and the building carries on without them: the vents, the compressor under the vending machine, the lights on the east side that stay on because nobody can find the switch for them.

i brought a book. i have not opened it. i walk instead, and by the third hour the walking is the whole job.

the carpet

19 august 1999 · 3:14 am · mood: precise

the carpet on level 2 repeats every 4.6 metres. i counted it in strides first and then measured it properly with the tape from the supply cupboard, which is how i know it is 4.6 and not 5.

for the first eight repeats you see the pattern. after that you only see the join, and once you have seen the join you cannot go back to seeing the pattern.

door 12

2 september 1999 · 3:14 am · mood: reasonable

there are two doors numbered 12. one on the west corridor, one on the north. facilities say the north one was renumbered during the refit and the plate was never swapped, which is the sort of answer that closes a phone call without settling anything.

i have opened the west one. i have not opened the north one and i intend to keep it that way, because the west one opens onto a corridor that is the corridor i was already standing in.

the clock

30 october 1999 · 3:14 am · mood: tired of this

the clock outside the west stairwell has read 3:14 since the refit. i reported it in june and again in september. the second time, the man on the phone asked me what time it was. i told him 3:14. there was a pause on the line, and then he said he meant on my watch.

y2k

1 january 2000 · 3:14 am · mood: fine, actually

nothing happened. the lights stayed on. the vending machine reset itself to 1980 and started handing out free drinks, which is the most eventful thing that has occurred in this building since i started. i took a lemonade, walked level 3, came back, and it had corrected itself.
