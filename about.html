Hi, this is 'Galaxy 981' (cringe - yes, but anonymous no less!) and my pissing corner of the net. I dump notes from whatever books & articles i'm reading then try to turn the disjointed mess into little posts so future-me (and maybe you) can find them again. It wanders into politics, history, sociology, pop stuff and philosophy.


          
Some would call a chunk of this 'woke propaganda'. that's fine. bring snacks.


          
Sometimes i get things wrong. sometimes i fix them.


          
The aim is a couple of updates a month (never more than once a day my brain needs a brake). Topics will hop around because mine do :D


          
Say hi if you feel like it and i hope you enjoy your stay!!
