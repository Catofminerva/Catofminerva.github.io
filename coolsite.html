Intellectual Stuff to Read Everyday !

my daily rounds ★ click around / see whats new / read one or two
Harper's
oldest monthly in america, still going. the readings section at the front is my fave bit, weird found documents n excerpts. also long essays on politics + culture. some free, some not
n+1
brooklyn lit + politics mag, a bit snobby but in a way i enjoy. print comes out 3x a year but they put online-only stuff up every week so theres always smth new
London Review of Books ← i read this a lot
you probably know this one, lrb is one of the most famous literary magazines for book reviews and essays and i just love to read it so why the heck not include it
The New York Review of Books
another notable literary magazine, the american competitor to the above (lrb wins...)
LSE Review of Books
free open access reviews of social science books, updated basically daily. great for finding academic books u had no idea existed. no paywall!!
Aeon
essays on philosophy, science, psychology, society. all free, all pretty digestible, most of them a 15 min read. their sister site Psyche does shorter psych/how-to-live stuff
The Nation
oldest lefty weekly in the us, running since 1865 (!!!). politics, reporting, culture crit, and they still print poetry which is kinda lovely
Times Literary Supplement
the tls, british, going since 1902. reviews of basically everything, from shakespeare to political theory. went fortnightly in print but the site updates constantly. paywalled tho :(
The Paris Review
THE literary magazine. their Writers at Work interview archive is legendary and a lot of it is free. the daily blog is good for a quick morning read
The Atlantic
big american mag, mixed bag honestly, but when its good its really good. mostly paywalled which is annoying
Jacobin
socialist mag, super readable and openly partisan about it. where i go when i want the class analysis take on whatevers happening. free!
Nautilus
science mag that treats science as part of culture instead of just Facts. physics, biology, consciousness, and the writing is actually beautiful
LessWrong
basically a forum for a bunch of contrarian nerds freaking out about AI ... BUT (!!) you often find some awesome user submitted articles on philosophy, tech, psychology, econ. etc that kinda think outside the box.
Opinio JurisLAW NERD CORNER
*The* blog on international law. I'm a law grad and specialised in intl. law in grad school - this is where i go when i need to brush up on stuff / look at latest updates in the field etc.
Margaret Killjoy
anarchist writer + musician + scifi novelist, writes on her substack about anarchism, forgotten rebellions, and how to actually live under all this. free to read, no paywall nonsense
Wired Magazine
mostly about how tech reshapes our everyday lives, politics, culture, with a lot of investigative stories on this, some very pessimisitc on the future others not so much
